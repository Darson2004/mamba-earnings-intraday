In this project, I am trying to build a algorithmic trading model that reads earnings data post announcement and predicts the intraday movements. To do so, I use the MambaStock model, which is deterministic, predict multiple times each minute for Medium Frequency Trading over the day. Currently, I am predicting the slowest times of the day, which is 11:00AM to 1:00PM, and will expand this range later. For strategy purposes, I want to buy specific stocks where the model deems a positive return greater than the purchasing and selling fees, and sell it when the model has reached near its predicted return or predicts a further decrease in stock price. To short specific stocks or leveraging the negative predictions made by the model, the model should again short when the predicted drop is greater than the purchasing and selling fees, and cover the short when the model has reached near the predicted drop or predicts an increase in stock price.

Note: open, low, float_share, close, pct_chg, high, volume, pe_nan_mask, pe, turnover_rate_mask, turnover_rate, total_share, float_share is the order from most important to least important according to the model
